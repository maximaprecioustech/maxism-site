Array Home Collections Case Study

Social media advertising & management for an e-commerce brand

Brief

Array Home Collections is a new e-commerce platform based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria that sells home furniture and décor.

They thus needed brand awareness within Port Harcourt specifically. And since their products were more pricey than the average due to being imported, they required not just any customer but potential customers who were in the income bracket to purchase such.

Strategy: “Black Valentine’s Campaign”

The idea behind the campaign was straightforward: offer enticing discounts for Valentine’s Day shoppers. We suggested a 50% discount on select products.

We then utilized Facebook’s unparalleled audience targeting to segment our target audience by location, age, income brackets and those interested in online shopping.

We further ran retargeting (remarketing) ads to put our ads back in front of visitors who did not make any purchase.

Lastly, we implemented Facebook’s Pixel tracking code to track the number of visitors who added to cart or checked out so as to effectively monitor conversions.

These ads ran on Facebook, Messenger, Instagram and Facebook Audience Network.

Results

Within a 2 week period:

34,784People based in Port Harcourt were exposed to the brand through social media ads.
50,185Times the ad was seen.
1,180Engagements with the ad (liked, commented & shared).
1,123People clicked the ad with intention to purchase.
₦900,000+Sales recorded by the company within just 5 days.
Staff trainingAll their staff members were trained in basic social media management.
3 videosCreated for social media.
